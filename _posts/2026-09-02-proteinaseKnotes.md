The Qiagen Blood and Tissue Kit ran out of Proteinase K... post details notes about what I can do moving forward!

# The Issue
I was given extra [Qiagen Blood and Tissue Kits](https://www.qiagen.com/us/products/discovery-and-translational-research/dna-rna-purification/dna-purification/genomic-dna/dneasy-blood-and-tissue-kit) from Olivia Graham from FHL for use in extracting DNA from mussel tissue. 

However, there was not NEARLY enough Proteinase K, which is an important component of the lysis process. 

# The Question
I have used Proteinase K in the past for my sea star coelomocyte extractions using the [Zymo Research Quick DNA/RNA Microprep Plus Kit](https://www.zymoresearch.com/products/quick-dna-microprep-plus-kit). I still have some extra in the freezer in FTR 213 from previous kits. Could I use that? 

# The Answer
I messaged the Qiagen Help folks and Shaula G responded to my question saying that I absolutely could use a different Proteinase K, and that the main thing to pay attention to is the concentration. The [Proteinase K from Qiagen](https://www.qiagen.com/en-us/resources/download/kithandbookandprotocol/hb-0956-002-1094085-ps-qiagen-proteinase-k-0315-ww) is 20mg/mL. 

The Proteinase K from Zymo is "Our Proteinase K can generally be used with other commercially available extraction kits. However, we cannot formally validate or guarantee performance with third-party kits. We recommend following the third-party kit manufacturer's guidance regarding Proteinase K concentration, incubation conditions, and any required inactivation or cleanup steps before proceeding with extraction."

# Moving Forward
So, it seems as though I can use the Proteinase K from Zymo Research, as long as I have it in the correct concentration (20mg/mL). Yay!
