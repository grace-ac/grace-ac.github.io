## Protocol notes: 
Extraction Kit: [Qiagen DNeasy PowerSoil Pro Kit (Cat: 47014)](https://www.qiagen.com/us/products/discovery-and-translational-research/dna-rna-purification/dna-purification/microbial-dna/dneasy-powersoil-pro-kit)  

Extraction Notes: [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yapjMaE8jN2JjSJzGdpa4m87HKaHB4lYdOcLujKzwUI/edit?tab=t.w1wnes3tckih#heading=h.1qqldt9y0eub)   

Protocol was modified by Becca Maher specifically for swabs from eelgrass tissue (see link to notes above).   

Eelgrass swab sampling protocol: [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15Y1ogjHQ8rRCv9VB0iyzydAmJdkucYw3m8x022UN8PE/edit?usp=sharing)

**Note on sample S_EVP_08**: during the extraction for step 7 where you transfer 500ul of the liquid to a tube that has 200ul of CD2... I have been having to remove the swab using sterile tweezers because otherwise the P1000 tip doesn't fit in the tube to access the liquid. For this one sample (S_EVP_08), I accidentally put the swab in the tube with the 200ul CD2 and threw out the tube with the 500ul of liquid. I realized my mistake immediately. I used sterile tweezers to remove the swab, then fished the tube out of the trash (was on top of the trash and on a kimwipe) and transferred the 500ul of liquid to the tube with 200ul of CD2. Everything else from then on was the same and normal for that sample and all other samples.
